# Novamart Tribal Knowledge

## 1. Summary
Novamart is an e-commerce platform with evolving reporting and data processing pipelines. This document codifies the "tribal knowledge" required to understand how core financial metrics and customer analytics are actually calculated, highlighting discrepancies between different organizational consumers (e.g., Finance vs. Executive Dashboards) and documenting the historical debt embedded in the data warehouse (`novamart-warehouse`) and application codebase (`5ae1182`).

## 2. Why this project
As Novamart has grown, the definitions of key metrics like "revenue", "active customers", and "best sellers" have diverged based on the consumer of the data. Database schema evolutions (like the introduction of `order_lines`) and undocumented business rules (like excluded brands) have made it difficult for engineers and analysts to agree on the "source of truth". This document maps out these discrepancies to safely support future changes to reporting and ML pipelines.

## 3. Business understanding
At Novamart, the understanding of "revenue" depends on the audience. 
- **Finance** cares about the literal dollars processed and returned. Their numbers include all purchases (even test accounts) but meticulously subtract processing fees and chargebacks to arrive at a "Net Revenue".
- **Product & Operations (Dashboards)** care about legitimate, customer-driven sales. Their numbers exclude known internal test accounts, specific hidden brands ("lucente", "jetem"), and internal SKUs, but they typically ignore chargebacks. 
Consequently, the revenue reported on the Redash executive dashboards will never perfectly match the official finance statements.

## 4. Metrics

### 4.1 Revenue & Financials
- **Monthly Finance Statements (Source of truth for Finance)**: 
  - Generated by `novamart.jobs.monthly_statement` and materialized in the `novamart.statements` table, with final outputs exposed via the `novamart_analytics.statements_final` view.
  - **Gross Revenue**: `SUM(orders.price)` where `status = 1`.
  - **Net Revenue**: Gross Revenue minus payment processing fees (`payments.fee`) and minus `novamart_analytics.chargebacks`.
  - **Historical Quirks**: 
    - On Nov 20, 2019, the payment processing fee logic changed to include a flat $0.30 fee (`FEE_FLAT`), affecting expected net calculations.
    - An explicit fix for October 2019 fee calculation bug exists in `novamart_analytics.statement_overrides` view.
- **Dashboard Revenue (Source of truth for Execs/Product)**:
  - Calculated by `novamart.jobs.daily_report` (and `intraday_report`) and stored in `report_rows` and `report_rows_intraday`. Dashboards like `revenue_widget` and `daily_kpis` query these tables.
  - **Exclusions**: These jobs explicitly filter out `BRAND_DENYLIST` ("lucente", "jetem"), `EXCLUDED_SKUS` (1004856, 1002544), `analytics.test_users`, and statuses `0, 2, 3`. The `best_sellers` and `brand_revenue` dashboards also hard-code the exclusion of QA user `424242`.
  - **Schema Evolution (Dec 5, 2019)**: In commit `92596dc`, the database evolved to support multiple items per order via the `order_lines` table. Dashboards and reports were updated to read item-level revenue from `order_lines` (using a `UNION ALL` fallback to `orders` for older data). The `monthly_statement` job did not need updating because `orders.price` successfully maintains the total sum of the order.

### 4.2 Customer Counting
- **Active Customers (Board Deck vs. Daily KPI)**:
  - The `actives_board` dashboard defines active customers strictly: distinct users with at least one non-cancelled order (`status NOT IN (0, 2, 3)`) in the last 30 days, explicitly excluding `test_users` and users with QA-like or internal emails (e.g., "novamart.com", "example.com", or emails containing "qa", "test", "demo").
  - The `kpi_daily` job (which populates `analytics.kpi_daily`) defines it loosely: distinct users with `status = 1` orders in the last 30 days. It does **not** filter out test accounts or internal emails.
- **Registered Customers**: 
  - The `registered_conversion` dashboard reveals two isolated customer ID namespaces: the legacy numeric ID (`orders.user_id` / `users.id`) and the new UUID account ID (`accounts.account_id`). To accurately count full order histories for registered users, the systems must join `accounts` to `users` via the `email` column.

## 5. System
- **Machine Learning & Recommender Pipeline**:
  - The Similar Products API (`novamart.routers.similar`) serves recommendations by fetching cached scores. The cache TTL is 6 hours, but it automatically invalidates when the underlying BigQuery table's `MAX(updated_at)` changes.
  - It dispatches logic based on the `REC_MODEL_VERSION` environment variable.
    - `2.0.0` (Default): Uses `analytics.product_affinity_v2`.
    - `4.0.0` (ML model): Uses `analytics.model_scores`.
  - **Fallback**: If no scores exist, it falls back to top products from `analytics.trending_daily`.
  - **Training Data**: 5% of all traffic is served a completely random set of products. This acts as the unbiased training data arm (logged with `arm='random'` in `analytics.rec_decision_log`).
- **Fraud Detection**:
  - `novamart.jobs.fraud_score` runs nightly to score orders from the last 24 hours. The core score is `price / 3000`. This is boosted by +0.15 if the account is new (< 7 days) and +0.15 if the user has high velocity (>= 3 orders in 24h). If the score exceeds `FRAUD_HOLD_THRESHOLD` (0.85), the order status is updated to 6 (held).

## 6. Data
- **Refunds Unified**: Handled by the `novamart_analytics.refunds_unified` view. It merges cancelled/refunded `orders` (statuses 2, 3) with `payments` records that have negative gross/net amounts (gateway refunds).
- **Categories**: Category mappings change over time. The `category_revenue` dashboard uses a versioned taxonomy mapping (`analytics.category_names` unioned with `analytics.category_name_history` introduced in commit `33054cd`) to ensure historical orders are grouped correctly according to the category definitions active at the time of purchase.

## 7. Experimentation
- **Affinity v2 Job Bugs**: The `novamart.jobs.affinity_v2` job produces the default recommendations. It weights co-carts as 1 and conversions as 3. However, it contains two notable bugs:
  1. **Seasonality Bug**: It attempts to apply a monthly seasonal multiplier from `SEASONAL_FACTORS`. This list only has 11 elements, causing December (month 12) to fallback to a 1.0 multiplier.
  2. **Conversion Bug**: It determines conversion by querying `orders.product_id`. Because of the `order_lines` migration in Dec 2019, newer multi-item orders will not attribute conversions correctly to this model since it misses `order_lines`.
- **Model 4.0.0**: A logistic regression model trained nightly (`novamart.jobs.model_train`) on the 5% random arm traffic, using features like item counts, base price, base popularity, and account age.

## 8. Glossary
- **424242**: A legacy internal QA/test `user_id` frequently filtered out in dashboard SQL.
- **EXCLUDED_SKUS**: Product IDs (1004856, 1002544) representing internal testing or staging products.
- **BRAND_DENYLIST**: Brands ("lucente", "jetem") purposefully hidden from daily operations reports.
- **Status Codes**: 1 = Paid/Valid, 2 = Cancelled, 3 = Refunded, 6 = Held (Fraud).
